## Interpretation, alternatives and confidence

HYP-0008 (all selected acquired candidates contain return) is weakened; HYP-0009 (at least one lacks recognizable return) is supported within the inspected sequence. Confidence is **moderate** overall: the positive Archive return is directly visible, while negative sampling may miss a very brief intermediate return. No hypothesis is ruled out. H1–H7 concerning historical mechanisms and related questions remain untested.

The strongest alternative is a very brief overlooked return or an edit/capture artifact rather than a faithful historical boundary. Similar mask/backdrop sequences do not establish independent captures. Cropping, watermarking, encoding and prior edits can account for differences. The result establishes which inspected file offers program-return context for future study; it does not authenticate either capture, explain the transmission mechanism, establish a broadcast-clock duration, locate anyone, identify a participant or infer motive.

Audio has not been transcribed or used to support any wording. Blind listening and period cultural-reference research remain waiting. No engineering specialist is needed for this limited visual file comparison; historical RF mechanism work requires dated documentation and qualified review.
