# Institutional records and holdings research

**Status: preparation guide only; no records inventory, release authentication, or holdings
confirmation has been completed by this file.**

Search existing agency releases before preparing new requests. Consider station, library,
broadcast-history, and collector holdings, but treat a directory or catalog lead as unconfirmed
until the relevant item/access is established. Preserve actual search repositories, exact queries,
filters/date coverage, unsuccessful results, and access barriers in the search register.

For future `inventory.md`, record SRC/CLM revisions, agency/custodian, identifiers, release letter
and context, pagination, completeness, exact evidence locations, restrictions, actual inspection,
confirmed holding/access status, missing record/question, and analytical value. Distinguish a
partial third-party copy from an authenticated complete case file. Verify extracted dates, names,
quotations, and OCR against the underlying pages before findings.

Contemporaneous reporting collection starts with a defined November 23-30, 1987 newspaper/trade
press search before expansion through 1988/later follow-ups. Compare page images with searchable
text where available. An inaccessible record or failed search remains a gap, not proof of
nonexistence. Draft targeted requests or neutral guides only for documented gaps, preserving
their unsent status and user-controlled external actions.

Confirm holdings/access before travel or paid research. Research spending is the owner's decision.
This guide sends no request, contacts nobody, and makes no availability or historical finding.
