# Neutral interview-guide preparation

**Status: preparation guide only; no person has been contacted, interviewed, or quoted here.**
The owner handles or explicitly authorizes interviews/outreach. A guide is an unsent research
product, not contact permission. Keep private contact details and uncorroborated personal
allegations in restricted working storage, outside findings/shareable reports.

A future `neutral-guide.md` should connect a documented evidence gap to neutral questions:

- What did you personally observe, and approximately when?
- Which monitor, receiver, feed, or distribution path were you observing?
- What action did you take, and what changed afterward?
- What did you learn later, including through reporting or discussions?
- Which surviving log, recording, correspondence, or other record could verify the account?

Separate direct observation from later technical interpretation and later exposure to accounts.
Preserve uncertainty, clock provenance, actual evidence inspected, and source dependence. Avoid
leading the witness toward a favored mechanism or person. Occupation, access, community membership,
cultural familiarity, and resemblance cannot establish participation. Do not use masked/distorted
face or speaker identification. No guide or retrospective recollection alone proves a mechanism.
