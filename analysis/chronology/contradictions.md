## What the comparison establishes

The dossier can identify which accounts differ and which text/image locations support each
reported figure. It cannot yet determine that different authors used the same phase boundaries,
shared an accurate clock, measured independently, or described an unedited common capture.
No actual duration, engineering mechanism, hypothesis exclusion, participant attribution or
novelty conclusion follows from this inventory.

The [image/text check](image-text-check.md) resolves specific transcription defects before their
use here. It does not authenticate the original publication/edition, validate a speaker's
technical explanation, or supply missing recording timing. Retrospective testimony and repeated
station accounts are not additional independent confirmations merely because separately published.
