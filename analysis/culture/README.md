# Period-reference research

**Status: methods guide only; no period-reference interpretation is established here.**
The catalog records exact words/images, source locations, dated availability, alternatives, and
distinctiveness. Do not infer involvement from familiarity, community membership, or possession.

## Evidence order

For recording-derived interpretation, first complete source T07's relevant comparison/transcription
and required blind review, then perform T08. Verify the underlying word/image or retain unresolved
status. Preliminary public-availability work may proceed independently under a separate research
TASK after foundation review; label entries preliminary and do not count them as recording
interpretation or completed T08.

Compare claimed references with material demonstrably available before November 22, 1987. Preserve
publication precision, exact source/claim revisions and page/time/frame locations, inspected image
versus OCR/text scope, and access limits. Check extracted dates, names, and quotations against the
underlying material before findings. A later retrospective alone does not establish pre-incident
availability.

## Context and alternatives

Consider broadcaster/station references, advertising, television programs, music, masks, props,
video/radio equipment, technical knowledge, broadcasting, amateur radio, video production, college
media, computer/BBS communities, and prank culture where relevant. These are research contexts,
not involvement indicators. Assess ordinary public availability and distinctiveness with reasons.
Keep ordinary audience familiarity, parody, grievance, and other motive alternatives open until
evidence distinguishes them.

Use `reference-catalog.md` for actual future entries, with source-linked evidence or unresolved
status, limits, and next actions. Preserve negative searches without converting them into claims
that material was unavailable. No catalog scaffold or plausible resemblance establishes a finding.
