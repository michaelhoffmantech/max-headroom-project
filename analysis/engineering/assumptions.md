## Separate roles and necessary conditions

The source performance/video, playback equipment, intruding transmission point and operators are
separate unknowns. Official station tower endpoints do not locate any of them. H4's production
timing and H5's possible common intruder transmitting site can coexist with H1-H3's entry-point
questions. A video set or visible edit supplies no automatic transmitter-site conclusion.

H1 needs an affected link receiver/path; H2 needs an insertion route to the affected program
chain; H3 needs a downstream branch capable of the observed distribution. Their necessary
conditions require dated topology and path-specific evidence. Current missing records leave
those conditions untested. A ruled_out change would require an evidenced failed condition,
assumptions and scope in the canonical hypothesis register.

Next work should target the highest-value missing document or feed observation. If a later
calculation is justified, record its input revisions, justified parameter ranges, tool/version and
offline method before interpreting the result. Reassess the historical-record branch after its
timebox; an access barrier remains a named wait, not a historical conclusion.
