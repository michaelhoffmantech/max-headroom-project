# Bounded analytical cycles

**Status: operating guide only; no actual cycle or test is completed by this file.**
Cycle JSON records are canonical; Markdown preserves separately reviewable observations, method,
and reasoning. Follow the data-model and report contracts under `specs/001-investigation-dossier/`.

## Choose and freeze one question

Select an attainable question from the real-source baseline. Before any new query or test for
that cycle, preserve one immutable plan payload, system freeze timestamp, and SHA-256. Include:

| Plan field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Question/scope | One bounded question and why its answer could distinguish alternatives. |
| Alternatives | At least the relevant competing explanations, not just the preferred one. |
| Necessary conditions | Conditions each explanation requires, including explicit unknowns. |
| Predictions | Expected observations that strengthen, weaken, or leave each alternative unresolved. |
| Evidence/dependencies | Exact input IDs/revisions, missing records/assets/tools/reviews, and protection needs. |
| Output/timebox | A concrete output and reassessment timebox; external waits tracked separately. |

Later predictions are dated exploratory amendments or a new cycle, never replacements backdated
into the frozen payload. A plan or start-analysis manifest is not an executed test.

## Preserve hypothesis scopes

H1-H3 concern the entry point: external studio-to-transmitter replacement, internal feed
insertion, and downstream substitution. H4 asks whether at least part was prerecorded; H5 asks
about a common transmitting site; H6 asks about insider-reference knowledge; H7 asks whether a
clue came from copying/encoding/interpretation. Different scopes can coexist. Edits alone do not
establish that the entire performance was prerecorded, and a video set does not locate a transmitter.

## Perform and challenge

Record actual query/test timestamps after freeze. Protect affected acquired originals before
analysis and retain exact input/derivative links, method, settings, versions, observations, and
result. Keep raw observations separately reviewable from interpretations. Challenge the strongest
alternative, source dependence, hidden assumptions, and evidence that could reverse the conclusion.

Hypothesis revisions retain prior status and reason. ruled_out requires an evidenced failed
necessary condition with assumptions/scope; missing records are not disproof. An unresolved
performed test can be useful. An unperformed or illustrative exercise cannot satisfy actual delivery.

## Review and handoff

Obtain actual separate findings review with fixed target revisions/hashes, reviewer identity,
evidence inspected, exposure to the favored theory, critique, decision, and specialist limits.
AI review is reasoning review, not independent factual corroboration. Preserve the cycle's result,
hypothesis changes, next action, stopped branches, and reopening conditions. Continue routine
authorized work after review; wait only on dependent missing input or user-only actions.
